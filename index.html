What Glean is

One platform for enterprise search, AI assistance, and agents

Glean is a Work AI platform that indexes everything your company knows — across every tool and team — and makes it instantly accessible. Employees get a single place to find answers, create content, and automate workflows, all grounded in your actual company knowledge and permission controls.

Glean Search
Unified search across Confluence, Jira, Slack, Salesforce, Teams, GitHub, and 100+ tools — with permissions enforced automatically. Find anything, instantly, without switching tabs.
Glean Assistant
An AI co-pilot grounded in your company's knowledge — not the open internet. Ask questions, get cited answers, draft documents, summarize meetings. Works in your existing tools.
Glean Agents
Pre-built and custom agents that automate multi-step workflows — RFP response, account research, release notes, competitive briefs. Built by anyone, run on a schedule or on-demand.

The sales use case

Where the conversation started — and why it's strong

The GTM org at Icertis faces a problem common to fast-growing enterprise SaaS companies: sellers spend too much time reconstructing context and not enough time with customers. Glean addresses this directly across three high-value workflows.

Customer / Account 360
A single view of each account — combining Salesforce data, past emails, call notes, Highspot assets, and Teams conversations. Sellers walk into every call and every renewal fully briefed, without the manual prep sprint.
Faster ramp on account transitions
RFP Response Agent
An agent that drafts RFP responses by pulling from past submissions, product docs, Highspot content, and internal knowledge — dramatically cutting response time and improving consistency across the SE and sales team.
↑ Quality and speed on RFPs
Renewal & Product Swap Guidance
As the Vera product repackaging rolls out, sellers need real-time guidance on which products map to existing customer contracts. Glean can surface the right recommendation in-context — from Salesforce, Highspot, and product docs — without manual escalation.

Why Glean

What makes it different from other AI tools

Permission-aware by design
Glean mirrors access controls from every connected system automatically. Sensitive contract data and internal roadmaps are only surfaced to the right people — no extra governance configuration required.
Enterprise Knowledge Graph — not just retrieval
Glean understands relationships between people, projects, documents, and tools across your org. Answers are contextual and company-specific — not generic LLM outputs disconnected from your actual environment.
Native Microsoft ecosystem fit
Glean connects natively to Teams, SharePoint, M365, and GitHub — the same stack Icertis is built on. Connectors are maintained out-of-the-box, with no heavy custom integration work required to keep them current.
Model Hub — no lock-in
GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama — teams choose the model that fits the task. Consistent with Icertis' own OmniModel strategy: the right model for the right job, with enterprise-grade security across all of them.
